PINPAL
save a spot navigate back to it or share the location to friend using google maps navigation
Tired of "I'm by the bench near the library"? Pinpal drops a pin and sends it to your friend so they can navigate straight to you with the help of google maps
Why Pinpal 
Meeting people into unknown areas can be confusing
Pinpal solves 2 problems
1.saves a confusing location so when you need to go back it'll be easy to navigate back
2.shares the saved location to a friend via whatsapp as a link so when he/she taps the link he/she will be guided by google maps to the saved location
"No more where is the place again or it is pretty confusing to find a way back"
KEY FEATURES 
"Save location"- this will grab your exact coordinates and saves them in your local storage you can name the saved location
              
"Saved locations"- here you will see your see four options 
                  1.Show on map-here you can see your saved location in google maps
                  2.navigate-here you will be provided a path back to you saved locaion
                  3.share-here you can share your location to someone so he/she doesn't find it hard to come to you when the location is confusing
                  4.Delete here you will erase your saved location
