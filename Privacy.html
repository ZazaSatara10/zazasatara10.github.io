Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Pinpal ("the App") is designed to help you save locations and navigate back to them using Google Maps. This privacy policy explains what information the App accesses, how that information is used to keep your privacy

Information the App Uses

  • Geolocation (Location): The App requests access to your device's geolocation to save your current location and provide navigation directions. Location access is requested so as to allow the app to function according to how it was programmed
  • Local storage: Saved locations (name, latitude, longitude, and timestamp) are stored locally in your browser using localStorage. These items remain on your local storage on which we can't access it
  • No account or server collection: The App does not collect or transmit saved locations to any server by default. All data stays on your device unless you manually share the location to someone

Third-party Services

The App uses Google Maps URLs to show locations and start navigation. When you open a map or navigation link, you are taken to Google Maps (web or app) and subject to Google's terms and privacy policy

Cookies and Analytics

The App may include a small analytics tracking snippet (Google Analytics) to measure usage and improve the application. This analytics data is limited and is used only for improving the application. Also we use google adsense. Google uses cookies to serve ads.

Security

Saved locations are stored locally on your device. If you share your device or clear site data, these items may be removed. Always use the App over HTTPS for geolocation to function correct

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can open an issue on the project's GitHub repository or contact the maintainer via the contact information in the repository.
