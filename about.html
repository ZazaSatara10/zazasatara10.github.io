About Pinpal

Save, share, and navigate back to your locations using Google Maps.

What is Pinpal?

Pinpal is a lightweight web app that lets you save your current geolocation to local storage, open that saved place in Google Maps, navigate back to it, or share it with friends (for exampl[...]

Key features

  • Save a location with an optional name.
  • Open a saved location in Google Maps.
  • Start navigation to a saved location (Google Maps web or app).
  • Share a saved location as a Google Maps link.
  • All saved locations are stored locally in your browser (no account required).
